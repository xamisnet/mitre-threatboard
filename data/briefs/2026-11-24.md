## 1. Supply chain: the trust model for signed packages just broke

Two supply-chain events dominate the window. **n01 (critical)** — the CHAINDROP worm hijacked the keyv maintainer account to backdoor 400+ npm packages (keyv, cacheable-request, flat-cache); a `setup.mjs` dropper exfiltrates credentials and self-propagates across ~1.3B monthly downloads. **n05 (high)** — TanStack's CI pipeline was hijacked to publish 42 malicious `@tanstack/*` packages carrying valid OIDC/SLSA provenance, the first documented case where signed packages can't be blindly trusted. Both land on **M1045 Code Signing** (verify provenance, don't assume) and **M1033 Limit Software Installation** (audit your lockfiles now), plus **M1043** to rotate build-host credentials.

## 2. KEV is live this morning — patch the exploited apps today

**n02 (critical)** — Outlook NTLM-leak zero-day (CVE-2026-124194) is the only confirmed exploited bug in November Patch Tuesday and is due today: block outbound SMB 139/445 (**M1037**) in parallel with patching. Alongside it, five more known-exploited vulns: **n03** Cisco Expressway hardcoded credentials (already breached four devices at a Russian org), **n04** Apache Roller deserialization RCE (CVSS 9.8, active), **n06** FreeSWITCH SIP injection (used in ransomware), **n07** Cisco BroadWorks XXE (active, due today), and **n08** UnrealIRCd. There is no compensating control for a confirmed exploited RCE — all resolve to **M1051 Update Software — patch now**.

## 3. Breaches, fraud, and what is still watching

**n09 (high)** — Vanderbilt University ransomware: 1M+ SSNs allegedly exfiltrated and campus WiFi, Okta and Brightspace taken offline; segment BYOD (**M1030**) and keep verified backups (**M1053**). **n10 (med)** — AI-assisted fraud at Indian banks and fintech is an emerging TTP that bypasses transaction monitoring, so tighten **M1047 Audit** and step-up auth. Still unconfirmed but patch-worthy: **n11** Ivanti Endpoint Manager pre-auth RCE (CVSS 9.8) and **n12** Windows AD DS RCE/EoP ("Exploitation More Likely") — both resolve to **M1051 Update Software** despite no confirmed wild exploitation yet.
