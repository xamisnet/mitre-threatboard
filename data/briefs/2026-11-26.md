## 1. Critically-exploited software (patch first)

**n01 · CISA KEV — MONAI Label + N-able MailAssure (due Nov 26).** Two actively-exploited flaws: an unauthenticated path traversal (→RCE) in the medical-imaging AI annotation server's PUT endpoint, and an SSRF in MailAssure's LDAP sync check. MONAI Label servers sit beside sensitive medical data; MailAssure serves MSPs and hosting providers. Patch MONAI Label to 0.8.6; MailAssure hosted is patched, Local Cloud operators must self-patch to 20261121.1943.

**n02 · Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938).** Critical heap buffer overflow in Oracle OpenJDK (Java SE, GraalVM on x86-64 with AVX-512) with working PoCs; GreyNoise reports ongoing exploitation. Java is everywhere — millions of servers, containers, cloud workloads. Apply Oracle/OpenJDK patches released Nov 17 and hunt for post-exploit activity via EDR.

**n03 · PHP2Shell (PAN-OS mgmt).** Critical PHP RCE chained with a kernel priv-esc for full root on exposed management interfaces (CVSS 9.2); Dream Research reports a campaign chaining both across 25+ servers. Perimeter firewalls are the pivot point — immediately restrict management-interface access and apply PAN-OS hotfixes.

**n08 · Microsoft Nov patch + OOB.** Emergency out-of-band update fixed licensing/activation failures the Nov patch itself caused, alongside 1,093+ CVEs including the exploited Outlook NTLM leak (CVE-2026-124194, KEV-added Nov 10). Bring all Windows systems to current build.
