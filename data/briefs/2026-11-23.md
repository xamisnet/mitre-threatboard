# Themes this window, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK rather than signature chasing:

## 1. Supply-chain and software trust keeps breaking at the source

**LiteLLM (n01, critical, KEV, exploited):** Upstream compromise of the LiteLLM PyPI package — 1.107.0 carried a credential-stealing payload that fires on Python startup via `.pth` and `sitecustomize.py`, and redistribution reached PyPI and official container images. This is a build-time supply-chain attack (`T1195`), so the fix is about artifact provenance: pin signed hashes, verify SBOMs, don't drift on `latest`. The **M1045 Code Signing** and **M1051 Update Software** controls are the load-bearing ones here.

## 2. Patch Thursday is live, and the KEV list grew again

**CISA KEV (n02, critical, exploited):** Four additions confirmed exploited in the wild — SAP NetWeaver, Cisco Expressway hardcoded creds, VMware vCenter auth bypass, and Apache Roller deserialization. All resolve to "patch this week"; there is no compensating control for a confirmed actively-exploited auth bypass on a management plane.

**November Patch Tuesday (n04, high, 1094 CVEs):** The big monthly haul, including CVE-2026-124194 (Outlook spoofing) with exploitation already detected. Windows/Outlook estates should treat the November update as mandatory, not optional.

**Other patches (n12, n13):** A Brotli decoder out-of-bounds write reachable via compressed data (no confirmed exploitation), and a Budibase unauth global-admin creation via tenant-confusion icon init bypass — both patchable, neither exploited yet.

## 3. Breaches: identity and police infrastructure under pressure

**FBI NCIC (n05, high, wild):** Attackers went after the National Crime Information Center police database — warrants, stolen vehicles, missing persons. Scope is unclear; it's the second FBI incident in two months. Privileged DB access (`M1026`) and least privilege (`M1035`) are the dials to turn.

**Entra Connect Health (n03, critical, exploited since Jul):** Exploited since 23 Jul, discovered 9 Nov, fixed server-side 19 Nov. Leaked admin emails, tenant IDs, domain names, license tiers — no passwords or tokens. For every Entra Connect Health tenant, this is the deployment to verify sync posture on.

**Bell Canada "agentic" breach (n06, high):** A pre-production Maximo system (telecom circuit provisioning) was the entry point; business contact info and IPs stolen. Bell describes it as AI-assisted — worth watching as an increasingly routine attacker tactic, not a one-off.

**Third-party data theft at JPMorgan/Visa/Mastercard (n07, high):** The N0n extortion group hit external service providers holding marketing data (names, emails). No payment infrastructure compromise — but it's a reminder that your risk is downstream of every vendor you hand data to.

## 4. Nation-state and global adversary posture

**Austrian Foreign Ministry (n08, high, wild):** Breached 12–13 Nov during a Microsoft global outage; staff contact details — including diplomatic staff abroad — exposed. Attribution is provisional, but the timing to an outage window is a pattern to note.

**Cairn AI in autonomous exploit (n11, med):** A Chinese-nexus actor's exposed Cairn AI pentest instance ran autonomous agents to find a real zero-day in an open-source medical imaging app and gain root — over ~48h, targeting East Asian universities. This is the AI-native threat surface your **Threat Intelligence Program (M1019)** has to feed.

**BADBOX 3.0 (n09, high):** The Android-TV ad-fraud botnet is back, peaking at ~40B fraudulent bid requests/day (>4% of CTV traffic), 3× VASTFLUX's prior record.

## 5. Priority order (how to read the controls table below)

1. **M1051 Update Software** tops the list — five different exploited-in-the-wild products (LiteLLM, NetWeaver, Expressway, vCenter, Roller) plus the 1094-CVE Patch Tuesday all resolve to "patch this week".
2. **M1026 Privileged Account Management / M1040 + M1057** — the NCIC, Entra and Bell breaches are all, at bottom, privileged access and data-flow problems.
3. **M1030 Network Segmentation + M1035 Limit Access** — a compromised pre-prod Maximo, a management-plane auth bypass: segmentation is how a compromised appliance stays contained.
4. **M1045 Code Signing / artifact provenance** — the LiteLLM case is exactly the supply-chain control that "updates" alone can't give you.
5. **M1019 Threat Intelligence** — the Cairn autonomous-exploit case is the standing reason to keep feeding a real intel program.
